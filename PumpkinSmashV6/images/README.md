# Image targets

The three original reference photographs are `image1.jpg`, `image2.jpg`, and `image3.jpg`. They are also listed in `manifest.json` and are compiled by `../target-builder.html`.

MindAR tracks these exact flat images; it does not recognize similar objects. The wall around the detailed ball in `image2.jpg` has few distinctive visual features. If its recognition is unreliable, keep the printed ball pattern sharp, well lit, and in view, or replace the target with a more distinctive high-contrast photo and rebuild the database.
