Deployment and troubleshooting

  1. Build using a modern browser over HTTPS or localhost. The bundled compiler and runtime dependencies are loaded from this project, not a CDN. Large target sets can take longer to compile and may require more memory.
  2. Move the downloaded targets.mind and target-manifest.json to this folder's root beside index.html. Keep every listed photo in images/.
  3. Publish the full PumpkinSmashV7 folder on GitHub Pages. Open the game on your phone, grant camera access, and scan one of the actual reference photos.

Tracking limitation: this tracks the exact flat photos, not similar real-world objects. Use sharp, distinctive, well-lit images for reliable tracking.

If an image fails to load, check its exact filename and case under images/. If MindAR reports poor target quality, choose a more distinctive image and rebuild both generated target files.
