      Data on this device

      Calibration

      Put on the headphones or speaker you play with. Press Start, then tap the pad (or press any lane key) exactly on every click.

      Import songs

      Choose audio files from this device. They are decoded and analysed locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

      Imported songs and their charts are saved in this browser's storage (IndexedDB) and are only available on this device and browser. Use Export charts to save a chart bundle, and import the audio again (optionally with the bundle) on another device.

      Name files Artist - Title.mp3 for nice metadata.

      Recompile chart

      Poor results? If notes feel twice as fast or slow, use ½× / 2×. If notes are consistently early or late, adjust the chart offset. For quiet or ambient songs raise sensitivity.

      How to play

      1. Notes slide down four neon lanes toward the glowing pads.
      2. Tap the lane when a note crosses its pad. Anywhere in the lane's column counts — no dragging needed.
      3. Hold notes have a glowing tail: keep your finger down until the tail ends.
      4. Chords are joined by a white line: tap those lanes together.
      5. Chain hits to raise the multiplier (×2 at 10, ×3 at 25, ×4 at 50 combo).
      6. Ratings: PERFECT ±45 ms, GREAT ±90 ms, GOOD ±150 ms (adjustable in Settings).

      Keyboard: D F J K. If timing feels off, run the calibration in Settings.
