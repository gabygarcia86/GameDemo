HOW TO PLAY

Your ninja skates on their own. Jump, trick, grind and land clean to bank points before time runs out.

Touch / Mouse

  • OLLIE button (or tap the left half of the screen)
  • FLIP stick: flick a direction in the air
  • GRAB stick: flick and hold to keep grabbing

Keyboard

  • Space: Ollie (also pops off rails)
  • Arrows: Flip tricks
  • W A S D: Grab tricks (hold to keep grabbing)
  • Esc / P: Pause   M: Mute
