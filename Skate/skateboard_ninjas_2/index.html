    HOW TO PLAY

    Keyboard & mouse

    • A / ← and D / →: change lanes
    • W / ↑ / Space: jump (again in the air for a trick)
    • S / ↓: slide (in the air: slam down)
    • J / left click: throw a ninja star
    • F: backflip (big points, but land it or you crash)
    • Esc / P: pause   M: mute

    Touch

    • Swipe left / right: change lanes
    • Swipe up: jump (again in the air for a trick)
    • Swipe down: slide
    • Tap the ninja star button to throw
    • Tap FLIP for a backflip: land it or you crash
    • Tall things: go around. Knee-high things: jump. Overhead bars: slide under.
    • Stars lock on to Karens and evil ninjas in your lane, or the next lane over if yours is clear. You carry 5 and they recharge.
    • Jump onto rails to grind (roll into one and you crash), hit ramps to launch, and chain actions to grow your combo multiplier up to ×10.
    • Clip the side of something mid lane-change and you just bounce off. Hit it head-on and it's game over.
    • Power-ups: magnet (coins), shield (survive one hit), star frenzy (unlimited stars).
